# Staff training — RAMKIN loyalty (one-pager)

**App:** Admin / staff site (counter tools)  
**Rule:** Customers never add their own stamps. You record purchases. The free drink card resets **only after you confirm redeem**.

---

## Sign in

1. Open the **admin** URL.  
2. Sign in with your **email + password** (staff account).  
3. Use **Counter** for stamps; **Rewards** for approve / reject / redeem.

If you cannot sign in, ask the owner — do not share passwords.

---

## Find a customer

**By phone**

1. Counter → enter customer phone (include country code if shown).  
2. Tap lookup. Confirm **name** matches the guest.

**By QR**

1. Ask the guest to open their RAMKIN dashboard QR.  
2. Scan with the camera tool, or paste the code if scan fails.  
3. Confirm name before stamping.

No match? Guest may need to register on the customer site first.

---

## Stamp (record purchase)

1. After a paid drink, open the customer on Counter.  
2. Tap **Record purchase** once per paid drink.  
3. Watch the stamp count go up.  
4. When the card is full, the guest is **eligible** for a free drink request — do **not** invent extra stamps.

Mistakes: stop and call a manager. Do not refresh-spam the button to “fix” a miss.

---

## Approve / reject (managers; staff only if owner enabled)

When a guest’s phone shows a **reward requested**:

1. Open **Rewards** (or the customer on Counter).  
2. **Approve** if the card is legitimately complete.  
3. **Reject** only with a short reason (e.g. “Needs manager review”) — guest can request again; stamps stay.

Approve does **not** give the drink yet and does **not** reset the card.

---

## Redeem (give the free drink)

1. Prepare / hand the free drink.  
2. On the approved reward, tap **Confirm redeem** / redeem.  
3. Guest’s card should reset to **0** on a new cycle.  
4. Old history stays for the owner — you do not delete anything.

Never redeem before the drink is given.

---

## Quick do / don’t

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Stamp once per paid purchase | Let guests stamp themselves |
| Lookup by phone or QR every time | Guess or reuse another guest’s card |
| Redeem only after the free drink is served | Reset / redeem early |
| Call manager on disputes | Share your login |
